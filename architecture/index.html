Our Architecture

One commitment, from which everything else follows: what Figoya keeps is not text — it is claims.

What Figoya keeps

A claim is an individual statement, made explicit, attributable, and connected to what it rests on. We aren’t going to walk you through how that is built. What follows is what it gives you.

  • Claims, not documents.

    A document that enters Figoya isn’t filed away as a block of text to be searched later. What it asserts is made explicit, statement by statement, and every statement keeps a route back to the paragraph it came from.

  • Every voice is kept.

    When ten documents assert the same thing, Figoya keeps one proposition and ten assertions of it, each with its own author and source. Nothing is merged away. The single clean answer you read is a view over that, worked out when you ask.

  • Relationships are recorded, not narrated.

    What supports what, what contradicts what, what has been superseded by what — recorded as structure rather than buried in prose. That is what makes what contradicts this? a question you can ask rather than a research project.

  • Confidence is carried, not implied.

    Every claim carries how well founded it is, so the answer can tell you — instead of sounding equally sure about everything.

From document to answer

  1. A document comes in

    What it asserts is made explicit and connected to everything already there. Not indexed for later searching — read once, properly, so that every later answer can be traced back to it.

  2. A question comes in

    It is answered from that structure, with a language model doing one job only: writing it up. Anything in the write-up that isn’t backed by a recorded claim is rejected before it reaches you.

  3. Something changes

    The change is carried through everything that rested on it. Which is why Figoya can show you what a change will touch before you make it, and what no longer stands afterwards.

How each of those works is ours. That it works is measurable, and we measure it.

Privacy and locality

The system works by structuring what you give it, not by learning from it. Figoya does not need to train on your data. It can run on your own infrastructure — on-premise, air-gapped, or inside a particular jurisdiction. Privacy is a property of the design, not a promise in a contract.

One foundation, many surfaces

The record is the asset, and every Figoya surface reads and writes the same one: Docs, Ingest, Create, People, Market, Chat. Domain configurations adapt it to a particular industry without rebuilding anything, and other companies build their own products on top of it through the Figoya API.

Our own work sits in it too. Figoya’s architecture, strategy and decisions live in Figoya, and that is how we work on it every day.
