# The full website — preserved, not published

**This branch is an archive. Do not publish it. Do not merge it into `master`.**

`master` carries a deliberately small site: what Figoya does, what it aims to do, and evidence
of progress against RAG. This branch carries the full version that preceded it — Home, Problem,
Solution, Vision, Architecture, Philosophy — kept intact so nothing has to be rewritten from
memory when it is time to say more.

## Why the site was cut back

Nat unpublished the site on 4 September 2026. Two problems, both structural rather than
cosmetic:

**It explained the method.** The Architecture page named the three pipelines and their stages,
listed the epistemic edge types, described slices, query-plan compilation and answer
validation, and gave the failure-mode coverage breakdown. The Philosophy page went further: it
set out the whole reasoning behind claims as the atomic unit — why not a fact, why not an
entity, why not a token — which is the argument, in full, for free. The concept of a claim
graph is not itself novel; argument mapping and provenance-bearing assertions go back decades.
But the leap from *entities and facts* to *assertions with an author, a confidence and
epistemic relations* is a philosophical one, and a graph-engineering team would not
necessarily make it unprompted. Handing them the framing is a real cost, whatever the prior
art.

**It published the plan.** The Vision page named the lead vertical, the first regulated
vertical, the regulation being targeted, the year-by-year sequence and the count of candidate
verticals. That is a route map. A funded competitor reads it and knows where to aim — arguably
more actionable than anything on the Architecture page.

Neither problem is fixed by better wording. The site was saying things a pre-revenue company
with no moat but a head start should not be saying yet.

## What replaces it, and when this comes back

The small site argues from results rather than method: claims, and proof of those claims,
tested honestly and published with confidence intervals. See `WEBSITE-BRIEF.md` on `master`
for the structure, the do-not-publish list and the vocabulary rules.

**This is a temporary measure, not a change of mind.** The material here becomes publishable
again when the head start is no longer the only protection — funding closed, customers signed,
whatever else makes an imitator's year of catching up worthless to them. At that point the
full argument is an asset rather than a gift, and most of it can go back up as written.
