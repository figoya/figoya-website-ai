Figoya at its simplest

At its simplest, Figoya is an AI that specialises in understanding uploaded documents, and delivering high quality output from them.

Invaluable for professionals who need accuracy and transparency from their AI, so they can stand behind the results they deliver to their clients.

This is not the same thing done better. Figoya takes a new architectural approach to the problem, which lets it do more and do it better: paragraph-level citations, conflicting opinions shown side by side, contradictions surfaced rather than smoothed over, and answers re-checked when something changes.

It works on your own documents — the body of work you’ve built up over years, or just your current project files and the third-party material you’re expected to incorporate accurately.

Sound familiar?

You’ve uploaded some files to an AI and asked some questions about them. Some of the facts and citations are missing, wrong or vague. You ask the same question the next day and get a different answer. Two of your own documents disagree, and it hands you one confident paragraph that hides the disagreement.

With Figoya, every sentence of an answer comes from those documents, linked to the paragraph it came from, so you can check it. Ask again next month and the substance of the answer is the same. Where your sources disagree, you see the disagreement instead of a confident-sounding guess at who is right. And when something changes, the answers that rested on it are flagged for re-checking.

Figoya at its most powerful

Apply the same principle to a whole organisation — millions of documents, emails and group chats, written by different people at different times — and you get organisational intelligence.

Organisational intelligence (OI) is an organisation’s ability to see every significant thing ever written in any document, email or group chat — and how it all fits together.

Business intelligence tells you what your numbers did. Organisational intelligence tells you what your organisation has actually said — and whether it still stands. It answers your questions with extreme transparency: no stone unturned, no contradiction hidden, no opinion ignored, no change lost.

Management theory has wanted this for sixty years. What was missing was a way to build it.

Sound familiar?

Nobody in a large organisation has read everything. A decision gets made on a document someone wrote three years ago that a later email quietly overturned. Two teams work to different versions of the same requirement. A supplier changes a specification and nothing downstream notices until an inspection. And when a regulator asks how a decision was reached, assembling the answer takes three people a fortnight.

None of that is a search problem. It happens because what the organisation has said is spread across documents, inboxes and chat threads with nothing connecting one to another. Figoya connects them — so the contradiction is visible while it still matters, the change is flagged where it lands, and the answer to how did we arrive at this? is a question you ask rather than a project you staff.

Measured, not asserted

It is built, it runs, and it is measured. Documents in, structure out, answers with their sources attached. We test it head-to-head against RAG on the same corpora and the same questions, scored by a different model family than the one that did the work, and we publish results with confidence intervals — the standard we would want applied to anyone else’s claims. Numbers appear here as they clear that bar.

And Figoya is built using Figoya. The project’s own strategy, architecture and decisions live in it, and that is how we work on it every day.
